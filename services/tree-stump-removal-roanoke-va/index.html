Clearing trees, stumps, and yard obstacles

Trees become a problem for ordinary reasons. One grew too close to the roofline, another is dying and dropping branches, or something has grown up in the corner of the lot you would rather be using. Whatever the reason, the goal is the same: get it out cleanly and leave the area in a usable state.

Tell us which trees are involved, roughly how big they are, and what is around them. Access, nearby structures, slope, and where the debris can go all shape the right approach, so we look at the actual site before recommending anything.

Stump removal and what the area looks like afterward

Taking the tree down and taking the stump out are two different decisions. Taking the stump out lets you replant or build over the spot; leaving it means working around it later. It is worth deciding up front, because it changes what the finished area can be used for.

Planning what comes next

Clearing a tree usually leaves a gap that needs something. Because we also handle the rest of the property, the same estimate can cover regrading the spot, restoring the lawn, reworking a bed, or using the newly opened area for a patio. See lawn care and landscaping for the planting side, or patios, hardscapes and drainage if you want to build in the space.

Common questions

Tree and stump removal FAQs

Do you remove the stump as well as the tree?

Stump removal can be included. Tell us whether you want the stump taken out or left in place, because it affects the approach and what the area looks like when the work is finished.

Do you clean up after the tree comes down?

Yes. Cleanup around the cleared area is part of how we approach this work. Let us know how tidy you need the space left and whether anything should be kept.

Can you take out a tree that is close to the house or a fence?

It depends on the location, size, and what is around it. Access, nearby structures, and the lean of the tree all matter, so we look at the specific situation before saying whether the removal fits our services.

What can I do with the area once the tree is gone?

That is a good time to think about the rest of the yard. Once an area is cleared it can be graded, replanted, or used for a patio or landscape bed, and we can discuss that in the same estimate.
