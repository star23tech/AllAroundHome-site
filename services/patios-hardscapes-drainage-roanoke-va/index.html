Patio and outdoor living projects

Maybe you want a welcoming front patio, a back patio beside a screened-in porch, or a smaller outdoor area that is easier to maintain than a deck. We help homeowners think through how the space will be used, where it should sit, and what needs to happen around it.

Projects can include front patios, back patios, patios for screened-in porches, fire pit areas, pavers, decorative stone, pea gravel, and small deck-to-patio changes. The right plan depends on the site, access, grade, water flow, and the finish you want. When the porch itself needs work as well, see our porch contractor services.

Walkways, steps, pavers, stone, and fire pit areas

Hardscape details connect the new space to the rest of the property. That may mean a walkway from the drive, outdoor steps on a grade change, a paver sitting area, a stone border, or a pea-gravel section around a fire pit.

  • Walkways and practical paths through the yard
  • Outdoor steps and transitions between levels
  • Paver, decorative-stone, and pea-gravel surfaces
  • Fire pit areas and small gathering spaces
  • Small retaining walls where the layout calls for them

Drainage, grading, resloping, and erosion help

Water should be considered before the patio goes in, not after it becomes a problem. If the area holds water, washes out, or slopes toward the house, tell us what you have noticed. We can assess whether grading, resloping, drainage work, erosion control, or dirt blockers should be part of the project.

Every yard is different. An estimate lets us look at the slope, runoff, soil, and nearby structures before recommending a practical approach.

Landscape finishing around the project

A patio can be well built and still feel unfinished if the surrounding yard is left torn up. We can discuss restoring and shaping the nearby landscaping, defining beds, adding stone or gravel, and tying the project back into the lawn.

Common homeowner questions

Patio, hardscape, and drainage FAQs

Can you build a patio next to a screened-in porch?

Yes, that is the kind of combined outdoor project we can evaluate. We will need to look at the porch, door height, grade, access, and drainage before recommending the patio layout.

Can you help with drainage before installing a patio?

Yes. Tell us about standing water, runoff, washout, or a slope toward the house. Drainage and grading should be considered as part of planning the patio.

Do you handle small backyard patio projects?

Yes. A project does not need to be a large outdoor-living build to be worth discussing. Call or send the details so we can understand the space and scope.

Can you replace a small deck with a patio or outdoor space?

Potentially. We can look at the existing deck area, access, elevation, and drainage and discuss whether a patio or another low-maintenance outdoor area fits the site.

Do you install pavers, stone, gravel, or walkways?

Yes. We work with pavers, decorative stone, pea gravel, walkways, outdoor steps, and related hardscape elements. Material choices depend on use, appearance, budget, and site conditions.

Can you help with grading, erosion, or dirt blockers?

Yes. We can assess resloping, yard drainage, erosion control, dirt blockers, and small retaining walls. An on-site look is important because the right approach depends on how water and soil move across the property.

Do you finish the landscaping around a new patio or hardscape?

Yes, landscape finishing can be part of the project. Tell us if you want the surrounding lawn, beds, stone, gravel, or planting areas repaired or reshaped after the hardscape work.
