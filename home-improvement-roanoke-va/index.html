Planning around the property

Common home improvement problems around Roanoke homes

Roanoke-area projects often connect the home exterior to a sloped or water-sensitive yard. The visible repair may be only one part of the job, so we look at what is happening around it before recommending a scope.

Exterior wear and unfinished repair lists

Weathered trim, aging siding, loose gutters, worn paint, and doors or windows that need attention often appear together. Grouping related exterior items can produce a more consistent finish and reduce the hassle of scheduling several contractors.

Slope, runoff, and standing water

On a property with grade changes, a patio, walkway, retaining feature, or landscape update should account for how water moves through the yard. Tell us where water collects, flows, or causes erosion so drainage and grading can be considered before finish work begins.

Projects that leave the surrounding yard unfinished

Exterior and hardscape work can affect nearby lawn, beds, access, or transitions. Because our work spans both home improvement and landscaping, you can include cleanup and landscape finishing in the same project conversation.
